# Privacy Policy for StreamPro

**Effective Date: January 1, 2026**

StreamPro ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our application handles information when you use our services on Android Mobile and Android TV.

## 1. Important Disclaimer (No Content Provided)
StreamPro is a **media player utility**. We do not provide, host, or broadcast any media content, playlists, or EPG data. All content accessed through the application is provided solely by the user (e.g., M3U playlists, XMLTV URLs). We have no affiliation with any third-party content providers.

## 2. Information We Collect

### a. User-Provided Data
All data provided by the user is stored **locally** on your device's private database. This includes:
*   M3U/M3U8 Playlist URLs and names.
*   Electronic Program Guide (EPG) URLs.
*   "Favorites" lists and "Recently Played" history.

### b. Local Settings & Configuration
To enhance your experience, we store local preferences:
*   UI Settings (Language, Accent Color, Theme).
*   Playback Settings (Buffer size, Hardware acceleration).
*   User Profile info (Nickname/Email - used for local display only).

### c. Device Identifiers
We access the **Android ID** (`Settings.Secure.ANDROID_ID`) to create a unique local profile identifier. This ID is used to maintain your local settings and history even after app updates. This data **is not** transmitted to our servers.

## 3. How We Use Information
The information processed by the app is used strictly for:
*   Providing core media playback functionality.
*   Managing and displaying your imported TV schedules.
*   Personalizing the application's interface.

## 4. Third-Party Services & Libraries
The application uses the following third-party components:
*   **Media3 (ExoPlayer)**: A media player library by Google.
*   **Coil**: For loading channel logos.
*   **Google AdMob**: (If applicable) Used to display advertisements. AdMob may collect technical data such as IP addresses or Advertising IDs to serve relevant ads. Please refer to [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

**Note:** When you stream content, your IPTV provider may see your IP address and device information. Please consult your provider's privacy policy.

## 5. Permissions
*   **INTERNET**: To stream online media and download EPG data.
*   **READ/WRITE EXTERNAL STORAGE**: To import local M3U files or export backups.
*   **WAKE_LOCK**: To prevent the screen from dimming during video playback.
*   **POST_NOTIFICATIONS**: (Android 13+) To show playback controls in the notification area.

## 6. Data Retention & Deletion
Since almost all data is stored locally:
*   **Deletion**: You can delete all data by clearing the app's cache/data in Android Settings or by uninstalling the app.
*   **Export**: Users can manually export their playlists if they wish to migrate data.

## 7. Children's Privacy
StreamPro does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. Since we do not provide content, parental discretion is advised regarding the playlists imported into the app.

## 8. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be updated on this page with a new "Effective Date."

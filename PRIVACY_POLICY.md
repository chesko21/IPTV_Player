# Privacy Policy for StreamPro

**Last Updated: January 2026**

StreamPro ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our application handles information when you use our services on Android Mobile and Android TV.

## 1. Information We Collect

### a. User-Provided Content
StreamPro is a media player. We **do not provide any content**. All M3U playlists, EPG URLs, or media files are provided by the user. These URLs are stored locally on your device's database to allow the app to function.

### b. Local Storage
We store the following data locally on your device:
*   Imported channel lists and groups.
*   Electronic Program Guide (EPG) data.
*   App settings (Language, Accent Color, Player Engine).
*   Your "Favorites" and "Recently Played" history.

### c. Device Information
We may access your device ID (Android ID) to personalize your local profile experience. This information stays on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

## 2. How We Use Information
The information collected is used solely to:
*   Provide media playback functionality.
*   Display TV schedules (EPG).
*   Remember your preferences and history.

## 3. Third-Party Services
StreamPro uses third-party libraries for playback:
*   **Media3 (ExoPlayer)**: Developed by Google.
*   **LibVLC**: Developed by VideoLAN.
These libraries may collect technical data (such as IP addresses) required to fetch the media streams provided by your IPTV provider. Please refer to your IPTV provider's privacy policy for more details on their data handling.

## 4. Permissions
The app may request the following permissions:
*   **Internet**: Required to stream online content and download EPG data.
*   **Read/Write External Storage**: Required if you choose to import/export local M3U files or backups.
*   **Wake Lock**: Prevents the screen from turning off during video playback.

## 5. Data Security
Since almost all data is stored locally on your device, the security of your data depends on your device's security. Clearing the app data or uninstalling the app will permanently delete all stored playlists and history.

## 6. Changes to This Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy within the application.
